# ZIP auditor repair history

This register records the three known source-only auditor repair failures before the successful final audit. The exact intermediate source snapshots and full terminal logs were not found in the campaign filesystem when this package was assembled; the register labels that gap rather than presenting reconstructed drafts as originals. Known failure text and source digests are recorded only where the original turn output provided them.

The final auditor source and eight-test suite are included in this package at the campaign-relative paths expected by the tests. `final-offline-tests.log` records the successful eight-test run; the earlier five-test output is preserved separately as a stale-layout attempt. The successful audit receipt pins the final source and test hashes and records the 757-member streaming verification.

See `auditor-repair-history.json` for the per-attempt errors, exact known hashes, repairs, and retention limits.
